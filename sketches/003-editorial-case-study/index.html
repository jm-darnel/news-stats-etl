The problem

Twenty-two outlets publish at wildly different volumes, with different byline conventions, URL shapes, and paywalls. "TODAY," "Guardian staff reporter," and <a href>-wrapped CNN bylines are all pseudo-authors — real metadata trapped in label noise. The question was whether I could reduce all of it to one clean schema without per-source hacks.

Daily article volume across the full window — peak of 5,458 on Nov 16, 2023.

How it was built

1

Config-driven ingestion

One sources.yaml drives every outlet. RSS/sitemap discovery plus trafilatura extraction, no per-source code.

2

dbt-style marts

Staging → conformed dimension tables (dim_outlet, dim_author) → aggregate views. The modeling layer is what a BI tool actually consumes.

3

Five-year backfill

Streamed ~800 GB of GDELT GKG, kept only 22-domain matches (~1–2 GB), filtered garbage dates, landed 2.95M rows locally.

4

Author resolution

Deterministic canonicalization (accent folding, pseudo-author removal) plus a one-off LLM pass to merge surname-block aliases — 71.6K bylines → 39.8K authors.
