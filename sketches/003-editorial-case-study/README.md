## Variant: Editorial Case Study

### Design stance
A Learn/Decide surface styled on the Notion system: warm-white alternating sections, whisper borders, narrative prose with charts woven in. This is the *writeup*, not the live dashboard — a different surface, correctly composed as story rather than tool.

### Key choices
- **Surface:** Learn/Decide — prose-led, one idea per section ("the problem" → "how it was built" → "what the data says"); a hero is correct *here*.
- **Typography:** Inter, warm near-black ink `rgba(0,0,0,0.95)`, warm gray `#615d59`/`#a39e98`.
- **Color:** Notion Blue `#0075de` as the single accent; charts monochrome-blue; warm-white section alternation for rhythm.
- **Layout:** Single narrow column (~760px), numbered build steps, metric band, figures caption-first.
- **Interaction:** Click a bar row to flash a confirmation check (demonstrates the *writeup* is also a living page).

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at:** Advertising — it *tells* the story a client reads, and maps directly onto the article the user will write himself.
- **Weak at:** Being a tool — no dense chart grid, no configurable filters; it is not meant to replace the dashboard.

### Best for
The eventual personal-site article / case study — sits *beside* the live dashboard (Variants 1 or 2), not instead of it.
